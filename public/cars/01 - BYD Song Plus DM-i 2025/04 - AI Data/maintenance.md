## Service interval framework
- value: Maintain per the schedule by odometer reading or time interval, whichever comes first; severe road/driving conditions require more frequent items. Period counted from purchase date. Before first maintenance, HEV-mode use ratio ≥50% during ECO running-in.
- source: S5 · file: BYD-Seal-U-DMi-Owner-Manual-EU-source-alias.pdf · page: 207, 210 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Engine oil & oil filter
- value: Replace every **12 months or 15,000 km** (both NA and turbocharged engine rows). Use BYD-original / correct-spec engine oil.
- source: S5 · page: 209 (interval), 220 (oil guidance) · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Air filter element
- value: Replace every **12 months or 15,000 km** (more often in severe/dusty conditions).
- source: S5 · page: 209 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Spark plug
- value: Replace every **45,000 km**.
- source: S5 · page: 209 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Fuel filter (non-integrated)
- value: Every **24 months or 30,000 km**.
- source: S5 · page: 209 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Charcoal canister dust filter
- value: Every **2 years or 30,000 km** (or upon frequent...; see manual).
- source: S5 · page: 209 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## EHS gear oil / transmission & reducer oil / transmission filter
- value: Check EHS gear-oil quantity at each maintenance; replace oil + filter element every **4 years or 60,000 km**. Transmission/front-reducer/rear-drive oil: every **4 years or 60,000 km**.
- source: S5 · page: 209 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Brake fluid
- value: Check every **24 months or 30,000 km**; level must sit between MIN and MAX on the reservoir; if at/below MIN, check for leakage and brake-pad wear.
- source: S5 · page: 208 (interval), 220 (level) · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Coolant (expansion tank)
- value: Check coolant level in the expansion tank every **24 months or 30,000 km**.
- source: S5 · page: 208 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Chassis / steering / suspension / HV-battery-tray checks
- value: Brake pipeline & hoses, steering wheel & lever, drive-shaft dust cover, ball pin & dust cover, front/rear suspension, and the power-battery tray/anti-collision bar/guard plate/explosion-proof valve — all checked every **24 months or 30,000 km**. A/C strainer & filter every 24 months / 30,000 km.
- source: S5 · page: 208 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Tires (wear & rotation)
- value: Check tire wear at each maintenance; rotate/transpose if uneven wear >2 mm or as needed; increase frequency in poor conditions.
- source: S5 · page: 208 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Tire pressure
- value: **250 kPa front / 250 kPa rear** (normal); raise to **270 kPa when towing a trailer**.
- source: S5 · page: 247 (250/250), 116 (towing 270) · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review
- notes: The exact value depends on the wheel/tire fitted to the Jordan trim — verify against the Jordan car's door-jamb placard before treating as final.
