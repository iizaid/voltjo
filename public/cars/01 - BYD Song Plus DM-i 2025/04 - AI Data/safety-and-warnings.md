## High-voltage (HV) safety
- value: Do not touch high-voltage components (e.g. battery assembly) or the orange HV cables. Bare/damaged wires must not be touched — risk of serious or fatal electric shock. Have BYD service handle suspected leakage.
- source: S5 · source file: BYD-Seal-U-DMi-Owner-Manual-EU-source-alias.pdf · page: 41–42 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review (Jordan)
- notes: HV service is for qualified technicians only.

- value: 12 V battery fluid may be electrolyte — if leaked fluid contacts skin/eyes, flush with plenty of water (preferably boric acid solution) and seek medical attention. Do not touch leaked liquid.
- source: S5 · page: 42 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

- value: When leaving the vehicle, turn off the powertrain and press P; the car can move under motor power even with the engine silent if left in R/D without brake.
- source: S5 · page: 41 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Charging safety
- value: Charging equipment is a high-voltage appliance — keep children away; never operate with wet hands; do not touch the exposed metal of the charging port/base; do not repair the vehicle during charging.
- source: S5 · page: 97 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review
- notes: Pacemaker/ICD users should consult the device maker before charging.

- value: Do not charge or touch the vehicle in thunderstorm/lightning weather; do not shake the charger; do not touch the metal connection of port/charger/plug; use only certified equipment (uncertified products prohibited).
- source: S5 · page: 97 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

- value: If the charging port hatch/charger is frozen, do not force it open or pull the charger. Ensure no foreign objects in charger/port; insert the charger fully and check it is seated. A/C can run while charging (but turning it off improves charging power).
- source: S5 · page: 98 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

- value: To unplug: stop charging first, ensure the port is unlocked, then remove the connector; never force the charger out while locked. Refit the port protective cover and close the hatch after use.
- source: S5 · page: 99 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

- value: Extreme battery temperature reduces charging performance — in cold regions charge indoors with heating; in hot regions charge in a cool, ventilated place. Charge when the SOC bar reaches the red area.
- source: S5 · page: 99 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

- value: Charging-fault troubleshooting table (battery too hot/cold → wait for normal temperature; cable not fully connected → reconnect; power failure → reconnect adapter after power restored; persistent fault → contact BYD authorized dealer).
- source: S5 · page: 100 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Towing
- value: Adjust tire pressure for trailer towing; keep tires inflated to **270 kPa** when towing. Do not tow with a faulty or temporarily-repaired tire. 2WD model max total trailer weight figure given in manual (≤4,500 kg context). Observe local towing laws; do not modify the vehicle.
- source: S5 · page: 116 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review
- notes: At higher speeds the rear tire pressure must be raised above the normal recommended value (see manual).

## Jump-start / 12 V
- value: Jump start is for short-duration starting only — do not connect overcurrent for a long time. Do not substitute a higher-amperage fuse (fire/electrical-damage risk). Fill coolant with the proper tool; do not open the front fuse-box upper cover while filling coolant.
- source: S5 · page: 226 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Long-term parking / storage
- value: For long parking, use a garage or well-ventilated place; cover with a body cover in winter; choose a cool place for temporary parking. In saline/coastal/winter conditions, wash the chassis and wheel guards (high-pressure water/steam) at least monthly to reduce corrosion.
- source: S5 · page: 211 · market: EU export · confidence: official (EU) → needs_review

## Emergency notes
- value: For electric leakage, HV faults, or after a collision affecting the battery tray, stop and contact a BYD authorized dealer/service provider. Treat suspected HV fault as do-not-touch.
- source: S5 · page: 41–42, 100, 208 (battery-tray check) · market: EU export · confidence: needs_review
