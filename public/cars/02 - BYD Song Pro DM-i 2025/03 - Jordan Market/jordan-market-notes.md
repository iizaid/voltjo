# Jordan Market Notes — BYD Song Pro DM-i 2025

Source IDs → `sources.md` (P1–P8). Access date: 2026-06-04.

## Local identity
- **Common Jordan name:** BYD Song Pro DM-i 2025. [P1]
- **Not the same as:** BYD Song **Plus** DM-i (a larger, pricier model in the same showroom family). Keep records separate.
- **Aliases:** Sold as "Song Pro" (GL/GS) in Brazil; no Seal-style export rename found. [P6]

## Is it sold in Jordan?
- **Yes, present** — listed on Jordan marketplaces (Dooz, OpenSooq). [P1]
- **Channel:** appears to be **mostly used / parallel-import** listings. Unlike the Song Plus (confirmed *new* via the official dealer), a Song Pro DM-i *new* official-dealer listing was **not** confirmed this pass. [P1 vs P7 — needs_review]

## Dealer / importer
- **Bustami & Saheb (BSG)** is BYD's official Jordan dealer. [P7, dealer]
- Whether BSG offers the Song Pro DM-i as a **new** model (vs only the Song Plus) is **unconfirmed**. [needs_review]

## Price (Jordan)
- 2025 listings ~**20,500–22,000 JOD**; 2024 ~23,000 JOD (marketplace). [P1, owner_reported]
- Cheaper than the Jordan Song Plus (~26,900–28,900 JOD) — consistent with Song Pro being the smaller model.

## Trims in Jordan
- **Not verified.** Marketplace listings rarely state the China trim (75KM vs 115KM). → needs_review.

## Charging connector (Jordan)
- China Song Pro = **GB/T**. No official GCC/export Song Pro channel found, so Jordan units are
  **likely China-spec GB/T parallel imports** — but this is an inference, not confirmed per unit. [needs_review]
- **75KM trims have no DC fast charging at all**; only 115KM trims do. [P3] So a Jordan buyer's DC
  capability depends on which China trim was imported.

## Warranty / service
- **Unknown.** No Jordan warranty/service terms sourced; parallel imports often lack official warranty. [unknown]

## Verification status
