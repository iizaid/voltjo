# Trim / Name Equivalence — BYD Song Pro DM-i 2025

Source IDs → `03 - Jordan Market/sources.md`. Access date: 2026-06-04.

## First, the big one: Song Pro ≠ Song Plus
- **BYD Song Pro DM-i** = a **compact SUV** (~4735 mm, ~1635 kg, 52 L tank, 1.5L NA 101 hp, 12.9/18.3 kWh, CLTC range). [P2]
- **BYD Song Plus DM-i** = a **larger model** (~4775 mm, 60 L tank, higher system power, NEDC range; sold abroad as Seal U / Sealion 6).
- They are **different vehicles** in the same naming family. **Do not transfer any Song Plus spec, manual, trim, or connector onto the Song Pro.** This is the primary equivalence rule.

## Song Pro export naming
- **Brazil:** sold as **"Song Pro"** with trims **GL (12.9 kWh) / GS (18.3 kWh)** — name retained, no Seal-style rename. [P6]
- **No confirmed EU/AU "Seal"-style alias** for the Song Pro (unlike the Song Plus → Seal U/Sealion 6).
- **No confirmed GCC (UAE/Saudi) official Song Pro** listing — GCC BYD SUV lineup shown is the Song *Plus*. [R1, rejected for Pro]

## What can be reused safely (within Song Pro only)
- China ↔ Brazil Song Pro share the platform, body, battery tiers, and engine → battery/range *tiers*
  and body/engine transfer reasonably (still label market + standard).

## What must NOT be reused
- **Song Plus / Seal U / Sealion 6** data of any kind (different vehicle).
- **CLTC** range as if NEDC/WLTP/real-world.
- **DC fast-charge capability** across trims — it's 115KM-only.
- **Connector** across markets — China GB/T; Brazil/other connectors unconfirmed; GCC official Song Pro not found.

## Differences summary
- **Battery/range:** 12.9 kWh/75 km vs 18.3 kWh/115 km (CLTC).
- **DC charging:** 115KM trims only.
- **Trim names:** China 75KM/115KM 领先/超越/卓越 ↔ Brazil GL/GS ↔ Jordan (unverified).
- **Test standard:** China CLTC (others local/unconfirmed).
